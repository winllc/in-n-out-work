The download has all of it as JSON. Downloading is recorded in the audit log.

Who sees it, and for how long

Your times, notes and history are seen by you, your manager, your delegates, the managers of your groups and administrators.

Coworkers see who you are, and nothing of your attendance. see who you are and whether you are in or out today. see who you are, whether you are in today, and the day's status (telework, leave and the like).

Check-ins, statuses, requests and absence alerts are kept for 365 days, then summarised by month (days checked in, time in, days per status) and deleted; nothing before is kept in detail.

Check-ins, statuses, requests and absence alerts are kept until an administrator deletes them.

To have your data erased, ask an administrator. The audit log, which records who did what but not your notes, is kept.
