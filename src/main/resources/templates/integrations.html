Webhooks

Signed HTTPS posts when someone is not in by their expected time (absence alerts) and when someone's status changes. Each delivery carries X-ReadyRoom-Signature: t=<time>,v1=<HMAC-SHA256>; failed deliveries are retried with backoff. Only public addresses are allowed, and .

Webhooks are off: set APPLICATION_WEBHOOKS_SECRETKEY (32 characters or more, the same on every replica), which their signing secrets are encrypted with; with the Helm chart, webhooks.enabled and webhooks.secretKey.
