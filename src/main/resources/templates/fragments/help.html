How ReadyRoom works

ReadyRoom keeps track of who is in, who is away and who hasn't been accounted for, using the sign-ins you already make on your Windows workstation.

How check-in works

An agent on your workstation reports when you sign in, lock or unlock the screen, and sign out. You don't need to check in yourself: your first sign-in or unlock of the day counts as arriving.

What the statuses mean

IN Checked in and at your workstation.
AWAY Your screen is locked.
OUT Checked out: you signed out for the day.
NONE No activity recorded for the day.

A status you set on your calendar, such as work from home, TDY, leave or a late arrival, is shown instead, so people can see where you are even when you aren't at your workstation.

Finding your way around

Home
Your status today, your last 30 days and anything coming up. If people report to you, it also shows your team: who is accounted for, who isn't in yet, and alerts waiting for your response.
Groups
Browse the groups you can see and open one to see its members' statuses. Mark groups as favorites, then turn on Favorites only to see just those.
Users
Search for anyone by name (at least three characters) and open their details.
My Reports
Everyone who reports to you in the directory, in one table.
Org Chart
The organization's structure. Open an organization to see who is in it.
Metrics
Organization-wide figures: the accounted-for rate, status mix, workstation agent coverage and activity through the day. Metrics show counts only, never names.
Settings
The organization calendar, including holidays, and the rules that turn directory attributes into the org chart.

Keep your profile up to date

Open My Profile from the menu at the top right to:

  • set your usual login time; without one, your average arrival time is used;
  • add additional managers who should be told if you're not in;
  • drag across days on your calendar to set a status, such as leave or work from home, ahead of time;
  • leave notes for the people who look you up.

If you're not in on time

If you haven't checked in by your expected time, your managers are notified. Your expected time is a late arrival you've entered for today, otherwise your login time, otherwise your average arrival, plus a grace period. Weekends and holidays are skipped, as are days covered by an excused status. Managers see these alerts under the bell at the top left and can record what happened.

Looking at another day

Pick a date in the System Date box at the top of the page to see how things stood on that day. Every page follows the date you choose for the rest of your session, or until you pick another.
